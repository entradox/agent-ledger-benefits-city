### Judgement items — decided

- **Scope, `/banks/*` + `/states/*`:** the "merge also re-grouped them into the B2 grid" premise
  is **not true of the artifact**. Both families render the prior `class="card"` layout and are
  byte-identical to the base (`/banks/chase` 10,835 B, `/states/tx` 24,944 B). No revert, nothing
  to escalate. Logged as a class-A defect on this card for asserting it without measuring it.
- **`.offer-kind` contrast:** `#6E6E68` on `#F1EFE9` at 9.5px = **4.462:1**, under AA's 4.5:1.
  `--muted` → `#6A6A64` = **4.733:1**; all other pairings only gain (5.443 / 5.208 / 4.942:1).
- **Glyph coverage:** `scripts/build-fonts.sh` no longer claims to cover every non-ASCII glyph in
  `src/`. It covers latin-1 + the punctuation the public pages render. `U+26A0`/`U+FE0F` are absent
  from upstream Archivo (653 upstream glyphs, no warning sign), so adding them to `--unicodes` is a
  silent no-op; they appear only in the `METRICS_TOKEN`-gated internal dashboard.
